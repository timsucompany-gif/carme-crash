中文 English

CARME crash（以下稱「本遊戲」）讓你把照片當成車上主駕和副駕業務的臉，再把撞擊的過程做成圖片或影片。為了讓大家玩得開心，又不傷害任何人，請遵守下面的規範。第一次放照片之前，本遊戲會請你同意這些規範；之後隨時可以在「設定 > 照片使用規範」再看一次。

可以放的照片

你自己拍的照片、你自己的照片，或其他你有權使用的圖片，例如取得本人同意的照片、授權允許你使用的圖片。

不可以放的照片

  • 裸露、色情或帶有性暗示的照片
  • 任何涉及未成年人的不當內容
  • 暴力、血腥、仇恨或歧視的內容
  • 未經本人同意的他人照片
  • 用來霸凌、騷擾、威脅或羞辱他人的照片
  • 侵害他人著作權、肖像權或隱私的圖片
  • 其他違反你所在地法律的內容

照片放在哪裡

照片只在你的手機上處理，存在本遊戲自己的儲存空間，不會上傳到我們的伺服器，我們也看不到。你可以隨時在遊戲裡把照片拿掉。詳見隱私權政策。

分享的內容由你負責

本遊戲做出的圖片和影片，只有在你按下儲存或分享時才會離開遊戲。要不要分享、分享到哪裡由你決定。分享出去的內容由你自己負責，請遵守法律和各平台的社群規範。

違反規範

違反本規範所引起的糾紛或損害，由放照片和分享內容的使用者自行負責。如果你發現有人用本遊戲做出的圖片或影片騷擾或傷害你，請直接向刊登該內容的平台檢舉；需要協助時，也可以寫信給我們。

規範變更

本規範如有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方的生效日期；有重大變更時，本遊戲會請你重新同意。

English

CARME crash (the "Game") lets you put photos on the faces of the driver and the salesman in the car, and turn the crash into pictures and videos. To keep it fun without hurting anyone, please follow these rules. The Game asks you to agree to them before you add your first photo; you can read them again at any time under Settings > Photo rules.

Photos you may use

Photos you took, photos of yourself, or other pictures you have the right to use, such as photos of people who agreed to it, or pictures licensed for your use.

Photos you may not use

  • Nudity, pornography or sexually suggestive photos
  • Any inappropriate content involving minors
  • Violent, gory, hateful or discriminatory content
  • Photos of other people without their consent
  • Photos meant to bully, harass, threaten or humiliate anyone
  • Pictures that infringe anyone's copyright, image rights or privacy
  • Anything else that breaks the law where you live

Where your photos stay

Photos are processed only on your device and kept in the Game's own storage. They are not uploaded to our servers and we cannot see them. You can remove them in the Game at any time. See the Privacy Policy.

You are responsible for what you share

Pictures and videos made by the Game leave it only when you tap Save or Share, and you decide whether and where to share them. You are responsible for what you share; please follow the law and each platform's community guidelines.

Breaking the rules

Users who add photos or share content in breach of these rules are responsible for any dispute or harm that results. If someone uses pictures or videos made with the Game to harass or hurt you, please report it to the platform where it was posted; you can also write to us for help.

Changes

If these rules change, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed; after a significant change the Game will ask you to agree again.
