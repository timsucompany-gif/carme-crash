隱私權政策
Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 5 日 · Effective 5 October 2026

中文 English

CARME crash（以下稱「本遊戲」）由 timsucompany 開發。本政策說明本遊戲會處理哪些資料、怎麼處理。

你選的照片

你放在主駕和副駕業務臉上的照片只在你的手機上處理，並存在本遊戲自己的儲存空間，方便下次使用。我們不會上傳、收集或分享這些照片。你可以隨時在遊戲裡把照片拿掉。

分享圖和影片

只有在你按下「存到相簿」或「存影片」時，本遊戲才會把分享圖或影片存進你手機的相簿；要不要分享、分享到哪個 App，都由你自己決定。

遊戲資料

最佳紀錄、設定（例如震動開關）和每天換照片的次數只存在你的手機上。

世界排名

本遊戲的世界排名在 Android 上使用 Google Play 遊戲服務，在 iPhone 上使用 Apple Game Center。只有在你登入這些服務時（手機已經有帳號時會自動登入，你也可以選擇不登入），本遊戲才會把你在這支手機上的最佳分數，以及從手機地區設定取得的國家代碼（用來顯示國旗），送到該服務的排行榜。其他玩家會看到你在該服務上的遊戲名稱、國旗和分數。這些資料由 Google 或 Apple 依照各自的隱私權政策處理：

  • Google 隱私權政策
  • Apple 隱私權政策

你可以在 Google Play 遊戲 App，或 iPhone 的「設定 > Game Center」管理、登出你的帳號，以及設定誰看得到你的個人資料。

廣告

本遊戲使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。AdMob 可能會收集並使用裝置識別碼（例如廣告 ID）、大概位置（由 IP 位址推得）、與廣告的互動，以及當機和診斷資料，用來顯示廣告、衡量成效和防止詐欺。詳情請見 Google 的說明：

  • Google 隱私權政策
  • Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式中的資訊

在歐洲經濟區、英國和瑞士，本遊戲會先透過 Google 的同意管理工具詢問你的同意，你可以隨時在開始畫面的「隱私設定」更改。你也可以在手機的系統設定裡重設或停用廣告 ID。

兒童

本遊戲不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

本政策如有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方的生效日期。

English

CARME crash (the "Game") is developed by timsucompany. This policy explains what data the Game handles and how.

Photos you choose

Photos you put on the faces of the driver and the salesman are processed only on your device and kept in the Game's own storage so they are there next time. We do not upload, collect or share them. You can remove them in the Game at any time.

Share pictures and videos

The Game saves a share picture or a video to your photo library only when you tap Save, and you decide whether to share it and with which app.

Game data

Your best score, your settings (such as vibration on or off) and the day's photo changes are stored only on your device.

World ranking

The Game's world ranking uses Google Play Games Services on Android and Apple Game Center on iPhone. Only while you are signed in to one of them (you are signed in automatically if your phone already has an account, and you can choose not to sign in) does the Game send your best score on this device, and a country code taken from your phone's region setting to show a flag, to that service's leaderboard. Other players see your name on that service, your flag and your score. Google or Apple handle this data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

You can manage or sign out of your account, and choose who sees your profile, in the Google Play Games app or under Settings > Game Center on iPhone.

Advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location (derived from your IP address), interactions with ads, and crash and diagnostic data, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See Google's explanations:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game first asks for your consent through Google's consent tool; you can change your choices at any time under Privacy settings on the start screen. You can also reset or turn off the advertising ID in your device's settings.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed.
